We formalize in Lean OpenAI's counterexample to Hadwiger's conjecture. For arbitrarily large $m$, we construct an $m$-vertex graph $G$ with independence number at most two and $h(G)<26m/75+2/3<m/2\le\chi_f(G)\le\chi(G)$, where $h(G)$ and $\chi_f(G)$ denote its Hadwiger number and fractional chromatic number.

We reuse OpenAI's partial Lean formalization of the matching-minor bound and its chromatic consequence, together with their later formalization of the graph construction. We adapt these proofs to Lax and prove the fractional version.
