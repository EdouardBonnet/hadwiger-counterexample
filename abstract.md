[The main theorem](https://laxarchive.org/lax-342547/Lax342547.Counterexample.html#s-Lax342547.Counterexample.arbitrarily_large_hadwiger_counterexample) refutes Hadwiger's conjecture. For arbitrarily large $m$, there is an $m$-vertex graph $G$ with independence number at most two and $h(G)<26m/75+2/3<m/2\le\chi_f(G)\le\chi(G)$, where $h(G)$ and $\chi_f(G)$ are its Hadwiger number and fractional chromatic number.

We include OpenAI's partial formalization of the matching-minor and chromatic bounds and their later formalization of the graph construction. We independently formalize entropy and graph-transfer lemmas, finite-field geometry, rank and probability estimates, and gradient realization. We also prove the fractional chromatic bounds and the final counterexample statements.
